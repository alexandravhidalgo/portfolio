The Design System

One system, two themes, and an AI that can build in it.

2 themes

Light and dark from one token sheet — no forked components.

Designed and launched a new design system for a legacy SaaS platform, balancing modern UI standards with the realities of incremental implementation. The system introduced accessible components, standardized patterns, and reusable tokens that were adopted across existing screens. 

Problem

Every new grid program shipped its own chips, its own table, its own empty state. Partners read this UI at 6am during a dispatch — inconsistency reads as unreliability, and every fix cost engineering a week.

What I did

  • Rebuilt from tokens: primary, secondary and tertiary roles on shared perceptual ramps, a real type scale, one chip vocabulary for every ISO and status.
  • Made dark mode a theme, not a filter — every surface, chart and status tint verified in both.
  • Wrote the system as machine-readable guidance so AI agents implement against tokens instead of guessing hexes.

Result

One vocabulary across Dispatch, Revenue, Analyze and the Incentives Portal. Product-wide design changes now land in hours instead of sprints.

2025—26 Design Front-end AI build

Incentives Gateway

A new revenue line, from a sketch to a deployed product.

$12.8M

Expansion opportunity the Impact Dashboard makes visible across 10 new regions.

Leap earns from grid programs. Partners kept leaving rebates on the table. I designed and built the portal that finds every incentive at an address, files the application, and bills for it.

Problem

Solar and HVAC installers close on price. Hundreds of rebate, VPP and carbon programs exist per address — buried in utility PDFs and county portals. Nobody owned that surface, and there was no product to sell against it.

What I did

  • Framed the wedge: one address in, every eligible program out, ranked by value — eligibility as the hero task, not a catalog to browse.
  • Designed and wrote the front end — marketing, pricing, auth, partner dashboard — shipping real code with AI as pair.
  • Modelled per-lookup and per-filing pricing with revenue, then built it into the page.
  • Built the Impact Dashboard: realized revenue against unclaimed opportunity, state by state.

Result

A side revenue stream that did not exist before, sold on performance-based pricing — 500+ programs in the catalog, ideation to live product with one designer.

2024—25 Research Data viz

Analyze & Revenue

What just happened to my revenue — and what do I do about it.

4 personas

One tab that serves an operator at 6am and a finance lead at quarter close.

After a grid event, partners had numbers but no explanation. I designed the tabs that name the cause, price the miss, and hand over the next action — in real time, while it can still be fixed.

Problem

A dispatch fires, meters underperform, money disappears — and the partner finds out weeks later in a settlement statement. Every one of those became a phone call with their Leap program manager.

What I did

  • Interviewed and modelled four partner personas — Conductor, Accountant, Operator, Developer — around what each needs in the first hour after an event.
  • Made missed revenue a first-class figure, not a variance buried in a report.
  • Designed Underperforming Meters: cause, confidence, dollars, meters affected and the next action, in one row.
  • Carried the insight into the dispatch email so the alert itself is the entry point.

Result

Partners self-diagnose after an event instead of calling. Underperformance became a ranked work queue with a dollar value attached to each row.

How I work now

The handoff was the bottleneck. So I stopped handing off.

Designing and building with AI changed what one designer can own. Not faster mockups — a different job description.

01 / DESIGN IN THE REPO

I prototype in code. Once I can open a pull request, the argument about whether it's feasible is over — and what engineering reviews is the real interaction, not a picture of it.

02 / THE SYSTEM IS THE API

A tokenised, written-down design system is what makes an AI agent useful. Vague style guides produce vague output; a precise one lets me change dozens of screens in an afternoon and trust the result.

03 / SHIP, THEN LOOK

Real partners, real dispatches, real money at 6am. I'd rather instrument a shipped screen and fix what the data says than perfect a flow nobody has used yet.
